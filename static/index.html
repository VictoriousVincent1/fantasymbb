Connect your ESPN league

Works with ESPN fantasy basketball points, category and roto leagues.

Open your league on ESPN and copy the address from the browser bar, or just the number after leagueId=.

Private league? Add two ESPN cookies

Most leagues are private. If yours is, ESPN only shares it with a signed-in member, so the coach needs two cookies from a computer where you're signed in to ESPN.

How to find them
  1. On a computer, sign in at fantasy.espn.com.
  2. Chrome or Edge: press F12 (Cmd+Option+I on a Mac), open the Application tab, then Cookies > https://fantasy.espn.com.
    Safari: turn on Settings > Advanced > Show features for web developers, then Develop > Show Web Inspector > Storage > Cookies.
    Firefox: press F12 and open Storage > Cookies.
  3. Copy the values of espn_s2 and SWID into the boxes above.

Your cookies stay in this browser. The server uses them only to read your league from ESPN and never saves them. They give access to your ESPN account, so only enter them on a device you trust, and click Disconnect to remove them. Trade Coach never changes anything on ESPN.
Read-only: this app never proposes or submits trades on ESPN. Estimates assume each active player plays one game, so they compare teams rather than predict a week's totals.
